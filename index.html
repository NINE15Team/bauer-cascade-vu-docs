VU Customizers

Maintenance documentation for the VU Custom 3D customizers on Peak Achievement Athletics: myBauer skates and steel, and Cascade Maverik helmets.

Division of responsibility

VU's main.js — hosted on their S3 — owns the 3D scene, recipe state, option sets, variant mapping, and the POST /cart/add.js. The theme owns the trigger, the modal, tabs, price, lead-time calculation, cart line attributes, and error tracking.

We cannot change the engine. Most 3D-level bugs are a vendor ticket, not a theme fix.

The two integrations

Same vendor, unrelated implementations. Different engine hosts, different shell code, different theme contract. Nothing learned on one transfers cleanly to the other except the vendor relationship.

Two things that will waste your time otherwise

Product templates and settings_data.json are not in git. Shopify keeps them in the Theme Editor. There is no {% render %} or section reference in the repo tying a customizer to a product — that binding is admin-side. The snippet is on the live PDPs.

Only Bauer CA was pulled. Other Bauer shops in this monorepo share _shared Liquid but their JSON and schema were never synced. Missing files there are expected.
